Strongest aspect: the manufacturer-anchored overall proportions and stable desk
pose are worth preserving.

Highest-impact defects:

1. Windscreen is a rounded box rather than a front-tapered, softly inflated foam capsule.
2. Rear shell is too rectangular; it should approach a rounded cylinder with a reduced rear cap.
3. Fork/mount is a dangling rectangular block rather than a compact molded joint.

Next changes: replace windscreen and shell with controlled superellipse lofts,
retain exact X/Z envelope, then chamfer and compact the mount around the pivot.
No taste decision is needed because the photographs establish the direction.
