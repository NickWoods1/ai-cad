# Iteration 001 visual review — revise

Compared the first contact sheet directly with the supplied cyclist photograph.

- Fidelity to brief/reference: **3/5** — the tall box, top flap, and piping read
  as a courier bag, but the proportions are too appliance-like and the right
  side omits the strong framed panel visible in the photograph.
- Primary silhouette and proportion: **3/5** — FRONT is too tall and narrow;
  the photographed bag feels broader, softer, and more weighty.
- Surface flow and continuity: **4/5** — the chamfered lower case and softened
  corners are clean, but the top remains rigid.
- Feature integration and transitions: **3/5** — piping is coherent on the
  front, while the first strap spline repeatedly sinks into the back pad and
  appears broken in REAR.
- Negative space and visual balance: **3/5** — the thin strap loops are useful
  from RIGHT, but their interrupted rear presentation looks accidental.
- Detail hierarchy and distinctiveness: **3/5** — the front wordmark and both
  logos are geometrically present but too faint and small in HERO/LOW.
- Ergonomics/apparent usability: **3/5** — inferred loops suggest wearability,
  but their interrupted shape does not yet look credible.
- Manufacturability: **4/5** — one valid solid; loop supports remain expected.

Preserve the faceted lower body and front piping. Widen and slightly shorten the
case, enlarge/project the branding, add right-side panel piping, and replace the
oscillating strap splines with controlled single-curve loops.
