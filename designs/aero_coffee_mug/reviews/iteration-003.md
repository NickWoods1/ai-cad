# Visual review: iteration 003 symmetric loft

## Comparison

The symmetric superellipse corrects the lopsided plan of the selected concept
and the 423.36 ml geometric cavity closely preserves the former capacity. The
body and handle now share a soft geometric vocabulary.

## Gate defects

- The front, rear, and hero views show horizontal bulges at too many section
  heights. The resulting body feels inflated and toy-like instead of composed.
- The handle's inner boundary intersects too deeply into the body. Hero and low
  views show pinched cusps at the upper and lower attachment roots.
- These are primary form and transition defects; secondary decoration would
  only distract from them.

## Decision

Visual Gate: **failed**. Reduce the body to six purposeful sections defining one
lower contraction and one shoulder gesture. Move the teardrop opening outward
while leaving the outer handle roots embedded, producing broader attachments.
