# Visual review: iteration 002 form exploration

## Compared variants

Three valid CadQuery concept solids were rendered through the fixed nine-view
review set. All retained the 95 mm body height and approximately the previous
envelope while replacing the rectangular handle and applied ornament.

- Concept A: restrained bell profile and compact oval ribbon handle.
- Concept B: stronger fourfold body rhythm and a taller teardrop opening.
- Concept C: broader streamline body and a smaller, lower handle.

## Review

Concept A establishes the most fluid transition from body to handle, but its
deep continuous waist reads as a vase or goblet. Concept C is calm and coherent,
yet the small handle and simplified body lose the desired Deco presence.

Concept B has the best hierarchy: the handle opening is memorable, its upper and
lower roots bracket the grip naturally, and the body retains enough controlled
geometric rhythm to carry the style without attached decoration. Its exploratory
harmonic plan is slightly lopsided in the top view, and the lower body remains
too generically goblet-like.

## Decision

Select concept B as the basis. Replace its phase-shifted harmonic sections with
symmetric soft-square superellipses, tighten the base transition, and retain the
tall teardrop handle. Continue to another render-and-review pass before adding
any secondary detail.
