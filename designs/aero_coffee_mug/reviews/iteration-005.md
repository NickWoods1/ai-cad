# Visual review: iteration 005 final refinement

## Comparison basis

- The original redesign brief and visual acceptance criteria in `SPEC.md`
- The blocky baseline recorded in iteration 001
- The three form studies and selected concept B recorded in iteration 002
- The latest nine-view contact sheet rendered from the validated STL

## Rubric

- Fidelity to brief and references: **4/5**. The soft-square plan, controlled
  waist, restrained pedestal, and arched negative space establish soft geometric
  Deco without returning to applied rectangular ornament. No external visual
  reference was supplied for a stricter image match.
- Primary silhouette and proportion: **4/5**. Front and silhouette views show a
  single continuous lower contraction and shoulder gesture. The handle is
  subordinate to the cup but remains characteristic.
- Surface flow and continuity: **4/5**. The six-section loft is calm across hero,
  opposite, and low views. The horizontal inflation visible in iteration 003 is
  gone.
- Feature integration and transitions: **4/5**. The outer handle roots remain
  embedded in the body while the inner opening now stays clear of it. The small
  boolean notches visible in iteration 004 are absent.
- Negative space and visual balance: **4/5**. The tall teardrop opening gives the
  handle a recognizable identity and balances the broad upper body.
- Detail hierarchy and distinctiveness: **4/5**. The body plan, vertical profile,
  and handle opening provide three related levels of form. Additional decoration
  would weaken the clarity achieved in this pass.
- Ergonomics and apparent usability: **3/5**. The rounded ribbon and approximately
  24.67 × 48.81 mm spline opening appear suitable for a compact two-finger grip,
  but comfort requires a physical prototype and may vary by hand size.
- Manufacturability and print orientation: **3/5**. The base is flat at Z=0 and
  the handle arch avoids a long horizontal bridge. Overhang behavior, watertight
  printing, and food suitability still depend on slicing, process, and material.

## Outcome

The strongest aspect is the relationship between the soft-square top view and
the vertically stretched handle opening: both are geometric figures relaxed by
continuous curvature. The design is notably less literal and less primitive-led
than the baseline while preserving the 95 mm height and approximately 426 ml
geometric cavity.

Visual Gate: **passed**. No unresolved material visual defect is visible in the
fixed review views. Physical grip comfort and manufacturing suitability remain
prototype assumptions rather than visually verifiable facts.
