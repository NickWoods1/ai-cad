# Visual review: iteration 004 simplified body

## Review

The reduced six-section loft removes the inflated horizontal waviness from the
previous pass. Front and silhouette views now show one lower contraction and one
calm shoulder gesture. The top view is a symmetric soft square, and the handle's
teardrop negative space supplies the strongest Deco note without applied bars or
bands.

The enlarged 47 mm-high handle opening improves apparent usability. However,
right and low views reveal small triangular notches where the rounded inner rim
still grazes the body surface near the two roots.

## Decision

Visual Gate: **failed narrowly**. Preserve the body and outer handle silhouette.
Shift the handle opening outward enough to leave an uninterrupted attachment web
after the 2.4 mm edge fillet, then rerender all views.
