# Iteration 001 visual review — accepted

Compared `output/doordash_logo/renders/contact_sheet.png` directly with the
user-supplied raster reference after a successful build.

## Rubric

- Fidelity to brief/reference: **5/5** — the TOP view retains the long tapered
  upper inlet, shorter lower inlet, rounded inner termination, and large outer
  return visible in the supplied mark.
- Primary silhouette and proportion: **5/5** — the reconstructed 120.000 x
  67.990 mm outline preserves the image-derived width-to-height ratio.
- Surface flow and continuity: **5/5** — cubic curves read smoothly in TOP,
  HERO, and OPPOSITE without faceting or curvature breaks.
- Feature integration and transitions: **5/5** — the inner slot and outer return
  remain part of one continuous silhouette.
- Negative space and visual balance: **5/5** — the horizontal slot has the same
  placement and rounded closed end as the reference.
- Detail hierarchy and distinctiveness: **5/5** — no bevel, border, backing
  plate, or secondary detail competes with the logo.
- Ergonomics/apparent usability: **not applicable** — this is a graphic solid,
  not a handled or body-contact object.
- Manufacturability/print orientation: **5/5** — one constant-depth solid with a
  broad flat Z=0 face and no support-requiring overhangs when printed flat.

## Decision

The strongest aspect is the smooth, faithful TOP silhouette. The orthographic
edge views confirm the requested uniform 8 mm extension, and the perspective
views show no unintended surface features. There are no unresolved material
visual defects. The visual gate passes on iteration 001.
