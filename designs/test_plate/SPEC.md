# Test plate specification

## Purpose

This is a pipeline verification model, not a functional component.

## Coordinate system

The plate is centred at X=0, Y=0. Its bottom face lies on Z=0. X is the 60 mm
width, Y is the 35 mm depth, and Z is thickness.

## Exact requirements

- Overall width: 60.0 mm
- Overall depth: 35.0 mm
- Thickness: 11.0 mm (revised from 8.0 mm to prove parameter-only iteration)
- Two through-holes: 5.0 mm diameter
- Hole centre spacing along X: 40.0 mm
- Hole centres lie on Y=0
- Plan-view outer corner radius: 3.0 mm

## Derived dimensions

- Hole X positions: plus and minus half the requested spacing.

## Inferred dimensions

None.
